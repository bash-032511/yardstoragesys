Who We Are

ECG Brokerage Inc. is a trusted logistics, customs brokerage, and container yard management provider dedicated to fast, secure, and transparent cargo handling. Our facility is engineered to streamline container storage, real-time yard slot tracking, and seamless Gate In / Gate Out operations for shipping lines, truckers, and consignees.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, accurate, and technology-driven container yard and brokerage solutions that minimize turnaround time and maximize cargo security.

Our Vision

To be a premier, modern container yard and logistics partner recognized for operational excellence, real-time visibility, and customer-first service.

Core Yard & System Capabilities

Automated Gate In & Gate Out Instant digital logging of driver, plate number, ISO size, and timestamps.
Live Yard Slot Locator 24/7 public and staff search to pinpoint exact container bay/block positions.
Dwell Time & TEU Monitoring Automated container aging alerts and real-time 1,000-TEU capacity tracking.
AI Yard Assistant Smart real-time inventory queries powered by live cloud synchronization.
